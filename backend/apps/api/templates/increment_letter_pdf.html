Revised Terms and Conditions:

I hereby acknowledge and agree to the following terms and conditions:

1. Acceptance of Revised Salary

I agree to the revised gross salary as mentioned in the increment letter and unconditionally undertake to continue my employment with the Company for a minimum period of one (1) year from the Effective Date, as defined in the increment letter.

2. Compliance with Company Policies

undertake to abide by all the rules, regulations, policies, and employment terms of the Company, as applicable from time to time.

3. Performance Expectations

I understand that the revised compensation is based on my current role, responsibilities, and performance. Any future growth in compensation will depend on my performance, contribution to organizational goals, and adherence to Company policies.

4. Client Confidentiality

I acknowledge that I may have access to confidential client information, including business data, contacts, processes, and commercial terms. I unconditionally agree to maintain strict confidentiality of such information during and after my employment and shall not disclose it to any third party without prior written consent from the Company.

5. Salary Confidentiality

I understand that my salary structure and compensation details are strictly confidential. I agree not to disclose or discuss such information with any internal or external parties, except where required by law or expressly authorized by the Company.

6. Notice Period Obligation

In the event of resignation, I agree to serve the required notice period as specified in my appointment letter from the date of submission of resignation.

7. Non-Compete, Non-Solicitation / Client Engagement Restriction

I acknowledge that, upon resignation, I shall not join with any competitors or commencing a rival business besides I shall not engage directly or indirectly with any customers, clients, suppliers, contractors of NeXHS Renewables Private Limited for a period of one (1) year from the date of my relieving, in any capacity that may conflict with the Company's business interests.

8. Exit Formalities

I understand that all exit formalities, including full and final settlement, handover of responsibilities, and clearance procedures, must be completed at the Company's Head Office in Chennai in person prior to my last working day.
